# AgentsHive Business US SEO launch checklist

1. Confirm production canonicals, titles, descriptions, Open Graph tags and JSON-LD on every service URL.
2. Verify `https://agentshive.net/robots.txt` and submit `https://agentshive.net/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.
3. Request indexing for the Business hub, Services hub and the highest-intent service pages.
4. Inspect indexing, selected canonicals, mobile usability and crawl errors after release.
5. Track impressions, clicks, CTR, consultation starts and qualified requests by landing page and US query.
6. Improve pages from actual query data. Add useful industry guides before considering location pages; never publish copied city or state doorway pages.
7. Earn relevant links through useful resources, partner mentions and real customer work only. Do not buy links or invent case studies.
8. Review claims, provider status and service availability whenever capabilities change.
